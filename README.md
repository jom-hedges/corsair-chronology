# Corsair Chronology

## Story
The player arrives in a forgotten world where objects
behave unpredictably. The goal is to travel throughout the world to collect different shards. Each region represents a particular era: the distant past where people still cling to vestiges of former objects; a golden age where people celebrate their lavish lifestyle; and an uncanny future.

## Setting
A central hub connecting different periods and environments.

## Puzzles
The player must collect certain items in different 
environments, and bring them to the central hub to trigger events in different environments.
